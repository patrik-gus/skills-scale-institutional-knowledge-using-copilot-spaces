# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme manages work through initiation, planning, execution, release, and retrospective. Each initiative starts with a one-pager defining the problem, measurable goal, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline, initial risks, and roles. Work moves into planning when outcomes and priorities are aligned and team capacity is confirmed.

Planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized, shippable backlog items with clear acceptance criteria, estimates, and a Definition of Done. Teams agree on milestones and release plans, identify dependencies, and maintain an owned risk register so uncertainty can be mitigated and monitored throughout delivery.

During execution, teams track work on a project board, deliver in small increments, and review progress through regular standups, weekly delivery syncs, and milestone demos. Quality is built in through unit and integration tests, CI checks and security scans, code review, and QA; teams communicate status and risks regularly and escalate blockers through the PM, Product Lead, and sponsor as needed.

Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers set outcomes and priorities; Developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Releases require acceptance criteria and CI/security checks, release notes, a rollback plan, and staging and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, and incidents turn lessons into owned, trackable improvements.
